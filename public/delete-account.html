Delete your Corpus account

You can delete your account and all of its data yourself, on the phone or the web. It takes a minute and can’t be undone.

In the app

  1. Open Corpus and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account and data.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete account and data.

Without the app

Sign in at corpus-fawn-eight.vercel.app and follow the same steps in Settings.

What gets deleted

Your login and everything saved under it: transactions, accounts, budgets, bills, jars, investments, money with people and settings. Nothing is kept afterwards. Want a copy first? Use Reports → Export before deleting.
